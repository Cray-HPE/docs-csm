# Change Passwords and Credentials

This is an overarching procedure to change all credentials managed by Cray System Management (CSM) in HPE Cray EX system to new values.

There are many passwords and credentials used in different contexts to manage the system. These can be changed as needed.
Their initial settings are documented, so it is recommended to change them during or soon after a CSM software installation.
